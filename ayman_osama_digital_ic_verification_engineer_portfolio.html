Core Profile

About Me & Verification Philosophy

Why digital verification matters: uncovering bugs in RTL before tapeout saves millions in fabrication resubmissions.

Transforming Specifications into Silicon Success

I am an Electronics and Communication Engineering student at Mansoura University with a rigorous focus on ASIC and SoC digital verification. I specialize in architecting modular SystemVerilog and Universal Verification Methodology (UVM) environments from the ground up.

My verification methodology emphasizes constrained-random stimulus generation, complete functional coverage closure using covergroups, and deterministic SystemVerilog Assertions (SVA) to pinpoint corner-case protocol violations long before physical tapeout.

Key Verification Directives:
Constrained-Random (CRV) SystemVerilog Assertions (SVA) UVM Testbench Hierarchy Functional Coverage Closure UVM RAL (Register Layer) RTL Timing & STA Basics

"Finding bugs in RTL early is orders of magnitude cheaper than catching them in silicon. Exhaustive verification is the foundation of high-yield hardware."

Corner-Case Discovery

Applying weighted constraints and automated randomization to stimulate edge cases that directed test vectors inherently overlook.

Coverage-Driven Verification (CDV)

Formulating explicit cross-covergroups and transition bins to mathematically track verification progress toward 100% sign-off coverage.

Assertion-Based Protocol Checking

Validating complex handshakes (valid/ready, burst boundaries) cycle-by-cycle using concurrent SVA for rapid failure localization.
