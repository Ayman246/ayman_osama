About me

I'm an aspiring Digital IC Verification Engineer focused on making sure hardware behaves exactly as specified before it ever reaches silicon. I build SystemVerilog and UVM verification environments — constrained-random testing, functional coverage, and assertion-based checks — to catch bugs early and prove designs are correct.

My approach combines curiosity, methodical thinking, and protocol-level attention to detail to uncover corner cases other tests miss. Through my training in SystemVerilog, UVM, SVA, and RAL, I've built a strong foundation for verifying complex digital and SoC-level designs.
